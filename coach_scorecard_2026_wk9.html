How the score works

Each team is ranked among all 138 FBS teams in eight categories. Each category is worth up to 100/8 = 12.5 points on a sliding scale by national rank, so a perfect team scores 100. Points slide smoothly between the marks below; the bottom quarter of FBS earns nothing.

Stuff
Defensive stuff rate: share of opponent runs stopped at or behind the line. Higher is better.
RZ off
Offensive red-zone success rate (EPA success on red-zone plays). Higher is better.
RZ def
Red-zone success rate allowed. Lower is better.
TO
Turnover margin per game: interceptions, lost fumbles and muffs. Turnovers on downs excluded.
ST
Special teams SP+, using SP+'s own national rank.
Pen
Accepted penalty yards per game (declined and offsetting flags excluded). Fewer is better.
2nd half
Second-half net EPA per play: offense EPA/play minus EPA/play allowed in the 3rd and 4th quarters. Higher is better.
Decisions
Win probability given up per game on 4th-down calls (go, punt or kick) and after touchdowns (kick or go for two), compared with the play-by-play model's best option. Regulation only. Lower is better.
