Terms

Edges
The team's offense against the opponent's defense, and the reverse, using percentile ranks among FBS teams. Top-third cutoffs are about +0.54 for stuff rate, +0.57 for red zone, +0.53 for early-down rush and +0.56 for opportunity rate.
Headline
The combined edge on EPA per play, EPA per dropback, yards per play and points per drive. Below +0.5 counts as "not agreeing."
QB void
If the QB of the team an angle is about doesn't start, that angle is void. If that leaves only one angle, the play is void and isn't graded.
Records
ATS against closing lines, 2016–19 and 2021–26 through Week 10, pushes left out. Break-even at −110 is 52.4%. Figures in orange are close to break-even.

These angles were picked after looking at the data, so expect real results below these rates, roughly 62% for card plays. The model uses play-by-play only, so it doesn't see injuries, QB changes or weather.
